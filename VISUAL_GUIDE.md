## 🎨 **Overall Visual Style**

**Color Scheme:**
- Background: Near black (#0a0a0a)
- Text: Light gray (#ededed)
- Accent: Bright green (#00ff88)
- Cards: Dark gray with subtle borders

**Typography:**
- Headlines: Geist Sans (Bold, 48-96px)
- Body: Geist Sans (Regular, 16-20px)
- Mono: Geist Mono (for stats/code)

**Animations:**
- Smooth fade-ins as you scroll
- Hover effects (scale, lift, glow)
- Animated counters counting up
- Typewriter effect on hero
- Rotating 3D diagrams
- Pulsing indicators

**3D Background:**
- Starfield with 5000 stars slowly rotating
- Floating green particles drifting
- Subtle depth and movement

## 📱 **Mobile Version**

On mobile (< 768px):
- Single column layout
- Larger touch targets
- Simplified animations
- No custom cursor
- Reduced 3D effects for performance
- Hamburger menu for navigation
